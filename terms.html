1. What the tool is

Wilde Fakten Uploader ("the tool") is a personal desktop application run by Patrick Bauer ("the operator") on their own computer. It creates short educational videos about animals and uploads them to the operator's own social media accounts, including TikTok as drafts via the TikTok Content Posting API.

2. Who may use it

The tool is used only by the operator. It offers no accounts, no sign-up and no service to other people.

3. TikTok

The tool connects only to the operator's own TikTok account, after the operator agreed on TikTok's authorization page. Use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Videos are sent as drafts; nothing is posted publicly without the operator's action in the TikTok app. The operator labels AI-generated content as required by TikTok.

4. Content

Video facts come from Wikipedia articles (CC BY-SA). Images come from Pixabay and Wikimedia Commons under their licences; credits are given in the video description where a licence requires it. Voice-overs are synthesised. Content is checked by the operator before it is published.

The tool is provided as is, for the operator's personal use, without any warranty.
