Wilde Fakten Uploader

Short animal fact videos – Kurze Tierfakten-Videos
Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Wilde Fakten Uploader is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on the operator's own computer and is used by the operator only. It creates short educational videos about animals, based on Wikipedia articles, and uploads them to the operator's own channels.

TikTok integration: the operator connects their own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit. Approved videos are sent with the TikTok Content Posting API (Upload Video, scope video.upload) to the operator's TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published automatically: the operator reviews each draft in the TikTok app, adds the caption, sets the AI-generated content label and posts it.

The tool has no public user accounts, no sign-up and no other users.

Deutsch

Wilde Fakten Uploader ist ein privates Desktop-Werkzeug für den eigenen Rechner des Betreibers. Es erstellt kurze Tierfakten-Videos auf Grundlage von Wikipedia-Artikeln und lädt sie als Entwurf in den eigenen TikTok-Account. Veröffentlicht wird erst, wenn der Betreiber den Entwurf in der TikTok-App freigibt.
